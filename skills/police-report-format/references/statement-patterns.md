# Record Patterns

Pre-reads: [definitions.md](definitions.md) for the Observed/Reported
distinction.

Patterns turn raw facts into finished field values. When the user supplies
the ingredients a pattern calls for, COMPOSE the value per the pattern — do
not wait for them to phrase it themselves.

## Narrative entry (PA11)

> On **\<date/time\>**, **\<what the officer observed or was dispatched
> to\>**. **\<Each subsequent action or observation, in the order it
> happened\>**. **\<What each party reported, attributed to them\>**.

Past tense, chronological, one event per sentence. Keep observed facts and
reported statements distinct. Example: "On 2026-06-02 at 07:14, I was
dispatched to 118 Maple St on a burglary report. I observed the rear
sliding door pried open and glass on the kitchen floor. The complainant,
R. Osei, stated she left the residence secure at 22:00 the prior night."

Ingredients: the timeline, what was observed, what was reported and by whom.

## Party record (PA8, PA9, PA10)

> **\<Role: Complainant / Victim / Suspect / Witness\>** — **\<name or
> description\>**\[, DOB **\<date\>**\]\[, **\<contact method\>**\]
> \[; **\<relationship to the incident or what they observed\>**\].

For a suspect with no name, a description substitutes ("male, ~180cm, dark
hooded jacket, fled east on foot"). Example: "Witness — T. Alvarez,
555-0148; saw two people leave the rear yard at approximately 02:00."

Ingredients: the role, an identifier or description, contact, and their
connection to the incident.

## Evidence / property record (PA13, PA14)

> **\<Item # / tag\>** — **\<description\>**\[, quantity **\<n\>**\]\[,
> est. value **\<amount\>**\]; **\<collected/recovered by, and custody
> note\>**.

Example: "Item 1 — pry bar, recovered from kitchen floor; collected by Ofc.
Rivera #4471, booked into property under tag 26-0455."

## Disposition statement (PA17)

> **\<one sanctioned disposition\>**\[ — **\<the one-line reason\>**\].

Example: "Referred — forwarded to the Detective Unit for follow-up on the
partial plate." "No exceptions"-style empty values do not apply here: PA17 is
always one of the sanctioned dispositions (see definitions.md).
